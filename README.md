# AI Naija Academy 🇳🇬
**Gamified, Mobile-First, Offline-Capable LMS for the ITF SUPA Artificial Intelligence Specialist (Level 3) Vocational Standard (27ITFVS26-3)**

*Training Window: Monday 19 October 2026 – Saturday 30 January 2027 (15 Weeks)*

---

## 🌟 Overview
**AI Naija Academy** is a learning management system engineered specifically for Nigerian youths and beginners with no prior programming experience. It prepares learners to achieve full competency under the **Industrial Training Fund (ITF) SUPA Artificial Intelligence Specialist (Level 3)** standard.

### Core Pedagogical Principles: "Understand Before You Build"
1. **The LMS is the Teacher**: Zero daily lecturer dependency. Every concept is grounded in relatable Nigerian contexts (Balogun market bargaining, Lagos Danfo and BRT fare stages, Third Mainland traffic alerts, Nollywood movie reviews, and Kaduna agricultural crop yields).
2. **20% Theory / 80% Practical**: Strictly enforced and verified in real time per learner and per cohort.
3. **100% Free & Zero-Card Prerequisite**: Powered by offline client-side WebAssembly, IndexedDB, and free tier tooling (Google Colab, Kaggle, Hugging Face).
4. **Resilient to Outages**: Offline PWA caching, IndexedDB local storage, and **Grace Tokens** that protect student streaks during power cuts or data outages.
5. **NDPA (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023) Compliant**: Minimal data harvesting, client-side persistence, and one-click data export/erasure.

---

## 🔄 The 7-Step Pedagogical Loop
Each daily lesson enforces an understanding gate before code is unlocked:
1. **Hook**: A 20-second real-world Nigerian dilemma or problem.
2. **See It**: Interactive visual simulation (memory boxes, decision tree branch routers, spine strain calibrators, confusion matrices).
3. **Predict**: Hypothesis guessing before execution with explanation reveals.
4. **Try It**: Guided mini-practical with Parsons drag-and-drop code blocks for phones and a mobile symbol toolbar (`tab`, `:`, `()`, `""`, `=`, `_`, `[]`).
5. **Break It**: "Find the bug" or "Stress-test failure mode" challenge to build intuition for bugs.
6. **Explain It**: Plain-English explanation evaluated against key semantic concepts, with built-in voice note / TTS support.
7. **Build It**: Mini-project capstone unlocking only after passing steps 1–6.

**Stuck Ladder**: Hint 1 (Concept) $\rightarrow$ Hint 2 (Code) $\rightarrow$ Worked Example $\rightarrow$ Flag to Facilitator Queue.

## 🗺️ 15-Week Naija Zones Curriculum
* **Zone 1 (Lagos Island)**: Welcome, OHS & Ergonomics, QA, Teamwork & AI Diagnostics (`A.1-A.5`, `B.1`, `C.1`)
* **Zone 2 (Ibadan)**: Python Core I: Variables, Memory Boxes & Conditionals (`D.1.1-D.1.5`)
* **Zone 3 (Benin City)**: Python Core II: Loops, Functions, Collections & Files (`D.1.6-D.2.4`)
* **Zone 4 (Enugu)**: Python Core III: OOP, NumPy Arrays & Vectorized Math (`D.3.1-D.4.2`)
* **Zone 5 (Asaba)**: Git, GitHub, REST APIs & Open Data Sourcing (`D.5.1-D.6.3`)
* **Zone 6 (Port Harcourt)**: Data Engineering, SQLite in Browser & ETL Pipelines (`E.1.1-E.1.6`)
* **Zone 7 (Calabar)**: Machine Learning I: Supervised Regression & Decision Trees (`F.1.1-F.1.6`)
* **Zone 8 (Abuja FCT)**: ML II: Evaluation, Confusion Matrix & Mid-Programme Defense (`F.2.1-F.3.1`)
* **Zone 9 (Jos)**: Deep Learning I: Perceptrons, Forward/Backprop & PyTorch (`G.1.1-G.2.3`)
* **Zone 10 (Kaduna)**: Deep Learning II: Computer Vision & CNNs on Cassava Leaves (`G.3.1-G.3.4`)
* **Zone 11 (Kano)**: Deep Learning III: Sequential RNNs & SMS Spam LSTMs (`G.4.1-G.4.4`)
* **Zone 12 (Sokoto)**: Transformers & Transfer Learning: Attention & Multilingual Tokenization (`G.5.1-H.1.3`)
* **Zone 13 (Ilorin)**: NLP Applications: Sentiment Analysis & Prompt Engineering (`H.2.1-H.5.4`)
* **Zone 14 (Abeokuta)**: AI Security, NDPA Privacy Auditing & Cloud Deployment (`I.1.1-I.3.4`)
* **Zone 15 (Victoria Island)**: Capstone Showcase, Revision & Official Mock SUPA Assessor Defense

## 🛠️ Portals & Dashboards
* **Learner Hub**: Daily missions, Naija map progress, XP, streak flames with grace tokens, squads of 5, and portfolio.
* **Tutor Intervention Hub**: Cohort analytics, real-time stuck learner queue, weak concept confusion matrices, and schedule adjustment broadcast tool.
* **SUPA Assessor Portal**: Frictionless read-only portal featuring the **~210 Criteria Coverage Matrix**, live 20/80 time auditor, and one-click **PDF Evidence Pack** generation.
